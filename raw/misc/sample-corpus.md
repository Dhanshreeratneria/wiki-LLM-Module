# Sample corpus note

This file is the nominal "source" for the ~120 sample wiki pages seeded into
`wiki/pages/` to demonstrate what a populated graph looks like in Obsidian.

The seeded pages cover real people, organizations, tools, and concepts from
the AI/ML field (Transformers, key researchers, major labs, popular
frameworks, etc.) with dense cross-links between them, modeled loosely on
the kind of knowledge base Andrej Karpathy describes building with the
raw/wiki pattern.

This is demo/seed data, not a real ingested transcript. Once you're ready to
build your own wiki, either:

- Delete `wiki/pages/*.md`, reset `wiki/index.md` and `wiki/log.md` to their
  empty templates, and start ingesting your own files in `raw/`, or
- Keep this seed set as a starting reference wiki and layer your own
  ingested content on top of it — the ownership rules in `CLAUDE.md` still
  apply either way.
