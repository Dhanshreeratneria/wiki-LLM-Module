# Knowledge Distillation

Training a smaller 'student' model to mimic a larger 'teacher' model's outputs, transferring capability at lower cost.

## Details

- Used to produce smaller, cheaper-to-run models that retain much of a larger model's quality.
- Often combined with quantization for further efficiency gains.
