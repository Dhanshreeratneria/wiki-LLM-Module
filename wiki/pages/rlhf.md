# RLHF

Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback: fine-tunes a model using a reward signal learned from human preference comparisons.

## Details

- Used to align base language models toward helpful, honest, and harmless behavior.
- A precursor to Anthropic's Constitutional AI approach, which reduces reliance on human labels.
