# ImageNet

Large-scale labeled image dataset and annual competition that catalyzed the deep learning boom in computer vision.

## Details

- Created under the direction of Fei-Fei Li.
- AlexNet's 2012 win on ImageNet is widely seen as deep learning's breakout moment.
