# Gradient Descent

Optimization algorithm that iteratively adjusts parameters in the direction that reduces a loss function.

## Details

- Stochastic gradient descent (SGD) and variants like Adam are standard for training neural networks.
- Learning rate schedules and momentum are common refinements.
