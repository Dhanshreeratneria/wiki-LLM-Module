# Ian Goodfellow

Machine learning researcher who invented Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs).

## Details

- Proposed GANs in 2014 as a two-network adversarial training scheme.
- Worked at Google Brain, OpenAI, and Apple on generative modeling and adversarial robustness.
