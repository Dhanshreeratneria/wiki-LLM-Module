# Elon Musk

Entrepreneur; co-founder of OpenAI and founder of xAI; CEO of Tesla.

## Details

- Co-founded OpenAI in 2015, later departed its board.
- Founded xAI in 2023, creator of the Grok model family.
- As Tesla CEO, backed the Autopilot/FSD computer vision program.
