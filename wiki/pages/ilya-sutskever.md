# Ilya Sutskever

Co-founder and former Chief Scientist of OpenAI; later founded Safe Superintelligence Inc.

## Details

- Co-authored the AlexNet and sequence-to-sequence learning papers.
- Was OpenAI's Chief Scientist during GPT-2 through GPT-4 development.
- Departed OpenAI in 2024 to found Safe Superintelligence Inc., focused on alignment.
