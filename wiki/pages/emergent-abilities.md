# Emergent Abilities

Capabilities that appear abruptly at sufficient model scale rather than improving smoothly.

## Details

- Chain-of-thought reasoning and in-context learning are commonly cited examples.
- A subject of ongoing debate about whether apparent 'emergence' is partly a measurement artifact.
