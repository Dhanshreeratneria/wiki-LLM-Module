# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator; former Tesla Director of AI and OpenAI founding member, now known for public deep learning courses and the 'LLM Wiki' knowledge-base pattern.

## Details

- Founding member of OpenAI, later led Tesla Autopilot's AI/vision team.
- Known for the 'zero to hero' neural network course and Eureka Labs.
- Popularized the raw/wiki two-folder pattern for personal knowledge bases built with an LLM agent.
