# AI Safety

Research area concerned with ensuring AI systems are reliable, controllable, and beneficial as capabilities increase.

## Details

- Spans near-term issues (bias, misuse) and long-term issues (loss of control, existential risk).
- Anthropic, DeepMind, and OpenAI all maintain dedicated safety research teams.
