# Demis Hassabis

Co-founder and CEO of DeepMind (Google DeepMind); Nobel laureate for AlphaFold.

## Details

- Co-founded DeepMind in 2010, acquired by Google in 2014.
- Led development of AlphaGo and AlphaFold.
- Shared the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for protein structure prediction work.
