# LLM Wiki Pattern

A personal knowledge management approach where an LLM agent compiles raw source material into a linked markdown wiki, avoiding embeddings or a vector database.

## Details

- Uses two folders: 'raw' (human-owned sources) and 'wiki' (LLM-maintained, cross-linked pages).
- Popularized by Andrej Karpathy as a lightweight alternative to RAG pipelines for personal use.
- Relies on an index page and bidirectional links rather than semantic search.
