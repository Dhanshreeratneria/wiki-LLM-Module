# Fei-Fei Li

Stanford professor; co-creator of ImageNet and co-director of Stanford HAI.

## Details

- Created the ImageNet dataset, which catalyzed the deep learning boom in computer vision.
- Co-founded Stanford's Human-Centered AI Institute (HAI).
- Founded World Labs, focused on spatial intelligence.
