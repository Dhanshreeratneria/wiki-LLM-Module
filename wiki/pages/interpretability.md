# Interpretability

Research area focused on understanding the internal computations of neural networks in human-understandable terms.

## Details

- Mechanistic interpretability aims to reverse-engineer specific circuits within a model.
- Anthropic's interpretability team, led by Chris Olah, has published extensively on this.
