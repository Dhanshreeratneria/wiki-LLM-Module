# AlexNet Paper (2012)

The paper describing AlexNet, whose ImageNet competition win is widely seen as launching the deep learning boom.

## Details

- Co-authored by Ilya Sutskever among others.
- Demonstrated the power of deep CNNs trained on GPUs at scale.
