# Noam Shazeer

AI researcher and entrepreneur; co-author of the original Transformer paper, co-founder of Character.AI.

## Details

- Co-authored 'Attention Is All You Need,' introducing the Transformer architecture.
- Co-founded Character.AI, later returned to Google DeepMind.
