AI researcher, professor at University of Montreal, and Turing Award laureate for foundational deep learning work.

## Details

- Co-authored foundational papers on neural language modeling and attention.
- Founded Mila, a leading deep learning research institute.
- Active voice on AI safety and international AI governance.
