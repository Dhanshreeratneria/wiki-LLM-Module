# Quantization

Reducing the numerical precision of model weights (e.g., 16-bit to 4-bit) to shrink memory and compute requirements.

## Details

- Enables running large models on consumer hardware with modest quality loss.
- Often paired with knowledge distillation for maximal efficiency.
