# RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation)

Technique that retrieves relevant external documents at inference time and feeds them into a model's context to ground its answer.

## Details

- Reduces hallucination by grounding responses in retrieved source text.
- Relies on embeddings and a vector database for the retrieval step.
