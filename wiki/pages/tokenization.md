# Tokenization

Process of splitting text into discrete units (tokens) that a language model consumes and predicts.

## Details

- Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) and similar subword schemes are standard for modern LLMs.
- Tokenizer choice affects cost, context-length efficiency, and multilingual performance.
