# Recurrent Neural Network

Neural network architecture that processes sequences step-by-step, maintaining a hidden state across time steps.

## Details

- LSTM and GRU variants addressed vanishing-gradient issues in vanilla RNNs.
- Largely replaced by Transformers for most sequence modeling tasks due to parallelization limits.
