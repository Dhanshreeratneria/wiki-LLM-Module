# Alignment

The research problem of ensuring AI systems pursue goals and behaviors intended by their designers/users.

## Details

- RLHF and Constitutional AI are two practical alignment techniques used in deployed models.
- Encompasses both near-term behavior shaping and long-term existential-risk concerns.
