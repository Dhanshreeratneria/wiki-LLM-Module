# Andrew Ng

AI educator and entrepreneur; co-founder of Coursera and Google Brain.

## Details

- Co-founded Google Brain, an early deep learning research group.
- Founded Coursera and later DeepLearning.AI for online AI education.
- Led AI initiatives at Baidu before returning to entrepreneurship.
