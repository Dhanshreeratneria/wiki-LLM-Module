# OpenAI

AI research and product company known for GPT-family models and ChatGPT; began as a nonprofit, restructured with a capped-profit arm.

## Details

- Founded in 2015 with backing from Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Ilya Sutskever, and others.
- Released GPT-3, GPT-4, and ChatGPT, catalyzing the modern generative AI boom.
