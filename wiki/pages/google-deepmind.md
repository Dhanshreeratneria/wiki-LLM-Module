# Google DeepMind

Google's unified AI research organization, formed by merging DeepMind and Google Brain in 2023.

## Details

- Led by Demis Hassabis; developed AlphaGo, AlphaFold, and the Gemini model family.
- Sundar Pichai oversaw the merger as Alphabet/Google CEO.
