# Chain of Thought

Prompting technique where a model is encouraged to produce intermediate reasoning steps before a final answer.

## Details

- Shown to substantially improve performance on multi-step reasoning tasks.
- A precursor to dedicated 'reasoning models' that use extended inference-time computation.
