# Neural Network

A model composed of layers of simple computational units ('neurons') that learn representations from data.

## Details

- Loosely inspired by biological neurons, though modern deep nets diverge significantly from neuroscience.
- Deep networks (many layers) underlie essentially all state-of-the-art AI as of 2026.
