# Transformer

Neural network architecture built on self-attention, introduced in 'Attention Is All You Need' (2017); the backbone of modern LLMs.

## Details

- Replaced recurrence with self-attention for parallelizable sequence modeling.
- Scales well with data and compute, underlying GPT, Claude, Gemini, and LLaMA families.
- Extended beyond text to vision (ViT), audio, and multimodal models.
