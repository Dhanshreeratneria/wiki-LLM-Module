# Geoffrey Hinton

Computer scientist often called a 'godfather of deep learning'; pioneered backpropagation for neural nets and later spoke publicly about AI risk.

## Details

- Co-developed backpropagation training for neural networks in the 1980s.
- Won the 2018 Turing Award jointly with Yoshua Bengio and Yann LeCun.
- Left Google in 2023 to speak more freely about AI safety concerns.
