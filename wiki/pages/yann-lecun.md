# Yann LeCun

Chief AI Scientist at Meta; pioneer of convolutional neural networks.

## Details

- Developed early convolutional neural network architectures (LeNet) for image recognition.
- Turing Award co-recipient with Hinton and Bengio.
- Vocal skeptic of pure autoregressive LLMs as a path to general intelligence, favoring world models.
