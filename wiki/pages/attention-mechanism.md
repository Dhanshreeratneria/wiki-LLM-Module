# Attention Mechanism

A mechanism letting a model weigh the relevance of different input tokens to each other when producing a representation.

## Details

- Originally introduced for machine translation to let a decoder 'look back' at relevant source words.
- Self-attention (used in Transformers) lets every token attend to every other token in a sequence.
