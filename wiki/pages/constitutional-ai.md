# Constitutional AI

Anthropic's technique for aligning models using a set of written principles ('constitution') rather than relying solely on human labels.

## Details

- The model critiques and revises its own outputs against the constitution during training.
- Reduces the volume of human feedback needed compared to pure RLHF.
